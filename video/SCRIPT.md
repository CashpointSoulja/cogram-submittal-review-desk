# Demo video script

File: `video/submittal-review-desk-demo.mp4` · 1080 × 1920 (vertical) · 30 fps · 79 s · H.264, no audio track

Zoom-ins land on the first flag and on the honest miss. On-screen subtitles carry the voiceover text below, so the video works muted. Times are from the recording log.
